Financing padel: purchase, leasing or joint venture?

Three ways to your own facility – and one of them costs you €0.

Not every club and not every operator wants to invest six figures straight away. And you don't have to.

Purchase. You invest once and everything belongs to you. The classic choice for operators with capital and a long-term plan.

Leasing. A strong option, especially for mobile platforms and vending machines. The instalments are paid from ongoing operations, and your liquidity is preserved.

Joint venture. With the Motions Racket Club, your club only provides the space. The joint venture takes care of construction, financing and operation. You receive a lease payment and invest €0.

Which route suits you depends on your goals: do you want full control, or predictable income without investment risk? We work through both sides with you.

Important: Always clarify tax and accounting questions with your tax advisor.
