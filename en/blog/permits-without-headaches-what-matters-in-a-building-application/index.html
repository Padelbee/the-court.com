Permits without headaches: what matters in a building application

Location, noise, lighting – the three issues where padel projects often get stuck.

The court is ordered quickly. The permit often takes longer. If you know the typical stumbling blocks, you save weeks.

Location and planning law. First question: what does the zoning plan allow on your site? On existing sports facilities this is often easier than on a greenfield site.

Noise. Padel is louder than tennis. Rallies off the glass wall are audible. Distance to residential buildings, court orientation and playing times should be clarified early.

Lighting. Floodlights extend playing time but need careful planning. We include light poles, colour temperature and alignment in the planning from the start.

Our tip: get the municipality round the table early. A good concept convinces faster than any objection.
