Padel at the hotel: the upgrade guests book

One or two courts turn your hotel into a sports destination.

Padel is the sport guests want to try on holiday. Easy to learn, sociable, perfect for couples, families and company groups. For hotels, that is an opportunity.

Little space, big impact. A padel court needs considerably less space than a tennis court. Even a single court is enough for a strong offer.

Usable all year round. With a hall made of polycarbonate or ThermoRoof, you play regardless of the weather. The hall can be extended as demand grows.

No staff needed. Rental machines hand out rackets and balls around the clock. Smart Access links lighting and access to the booking.

With your brand. Logo on the net, goal padding and court colour in your corporate design. That way the court becomes part of your hotel.
