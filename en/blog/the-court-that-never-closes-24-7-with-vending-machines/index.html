The court that never closes: 24/7 with vending machines

No staff, no counter, no problem. This is how your facility runs around the clock.

The biggest cost factor in operation is often not the court but the staff. If you put someone behind a counter to hand out rackets, it is hard to make the facility pay.

The solution: vending machines. They rent out rackets and sell balls, grips, snacks and chilled drinks. Fully automatic, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

What this means for you

  • Players book early in the morning or late at night – and find everything they need
  • Additional revenue with every ball and every drink
  • Remote maintenance, telemetry and software updates are included in the monthly fee

10 vending machines from 3 partners to choose from – from the ball vending machine for up to 100 tubes to the chilled combination box.
