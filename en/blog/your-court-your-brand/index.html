Your court, your brand

RAL colours, logo net, light colour – this is how a court becomes your court.

An off-the-shelf court plays well. A court in your design sticks in the mind.

  • Colours: Steel and details in any RAL colour, artificial turf in many colours, Mondo Premium Turf or the manufacturer's own turf.
  • Logos: Goal padding and net posts with logo, printed net and net band.
  • Lighting: Straight, C- or Z-shaped poles, adjustable colour temperature, additional LEDs.
  • Details: Bird-protection glass, wind load zones 1 to 4, indoor or outdoor.

For hotels, sponsors and clubs with a strong brand, the court is the best advertising space: every photo, every video, every post shows your logo.
