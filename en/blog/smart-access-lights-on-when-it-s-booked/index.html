Smart Access: lights on when it's booked

Access and lighting switch with the booking. No key, no wasted electricity.

We all know it: the floodlights are on, but nobody is playing. Or players stand in front of a locked door because nobody is there to unlock it.

Smart Access & Light Control links lighting and access directly to the booking system. Anyone who has booked gets in. The lights come on at the booked time and go off again afterwards.

Your benefits

  • No key handover, no staff on site
  • Electricity only when people are playing
  • Full control over access and utilisation

What it costs: €2,000 one-off, plus €49 to €129 a month depending on the number of courts. From 13 courts on request.
