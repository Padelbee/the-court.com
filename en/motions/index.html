FAQ

Questions about the joint venture.

What is the Motions Racket Club?

The Motions Racket Club is the operator in the joint venture with THE COURT. THE COURT develops the project, MRC operates the facility. Together we bring padel to the grounds of tennis clubs. The facility runs under the "Motions Racket Club [location]" brand, co-branded with your club. Our guide for club boards describes the whole process.

What does the joint venture cost my club?

Your club invests €0 and bears no investment risk. The joint venture takes care of the construction, financing and operation of the padel courts. Your club provides the space and looks after the maintenance of the outdoor areas and courts as well as the sanitary facilities. Always clarify tax and legal questions with your tax advisor.

How much lease payment does my club receive?

Your club receives a customary local lease payment per tennis court and month, agreed long-term. The exact amount depends on location and space, which is why we calculate it individually for your club. Instead of maintenance costs for an unused court, you get predictable income. More in Free tennis courts? Turn them into lease income.

How many padel courts fit on a tennis court?

Up to two padel courts fit on one tennis court. A padel court needs significantly less space than a tennis court. This way, an unused tennis court can become a small padel facility without your club needing new land. We check on site whether your courts are suitable, together with building regulations, noise protection and lighting.

What do club members get out of it?

Your members get a 30% discount on the hourly rate, at all MRC locations and not just at home. They can book 24 hours earlier than everyone else and get access to events, training and partner discounts. The joint venture is also building up programmes for children and beginners. More in 30% discount.

Who takes on which tasks?

The joint venture takes care of construction, financing and operation, youth and training programmes, tournaments at discounted rates and the brand. Your club provides the space in return for lease payments, looks after the outdoor areas and courts and the sanitary facilities, and anchors padel in club life. That way your club remains the host, but not the entrepreneur. Details in Who does what in the joint venture?
