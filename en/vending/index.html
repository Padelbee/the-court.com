FAQ

Questions about padel vending machines.

How does a racket rental machine work?

A rental machine hands out rackets without staff, in four steps: your players choose on the touchscreen or via the app, pay cashless by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or PayPal and leave a deposit. Then the right compartment opens automatically. After the game, the racket goes back into the compartment and the deposit is released. Details in Renting rackets without staff.

How much does a padel vending machine cost?

Our ten vending machines cost between €3,500 and €19,180 net. The Padel Ball Premium ball vending machine starts at €3,500, the Padel Premium 12 rental machine at €4,950 plus a €49 monthly base fee. The RentalBOX is available from €5,190. Some models come with an additional monthly base fee. All prices are net, as of 10/2026.

Which rental machine suits how many courts?

As a rule of thumb, for three to four courts a RentalBOX in size M with 23 compartments for €5,490 net is enough. The RentalBOX comes in sizes S to XXL and grows with your facility. With the slider in the RentalBOX configurator at the top of this page, you can see which size suits your number of courts. It can be combined with the DualBOX Lift for drinks, snacks and balls.

Can I finance a vending machine?

Yes, you can buy, lease or finance through hire purchase. Leasing starts at €109 per month, and the lessor remains the owner. With hire purchase from €104 per month, you are the economic owner from day one. Example: 48-month term, 20% final instalment, subject to credit approval. Always clarify tax and accounting questions with your tax advisor.

What is included in the monthly base fee?

The base fee covers telemetry, remote maintenance and software updates. This lets you manage your machine via app or display, remotely too, and you receive updates automatically. The amount of the fee depends on the model: for our machines it is between €29 and €59 per month, and some models have none.

How much does Smart Access & Light Control cost?

Smart Access & Light Control costs €2,000 one-off plus a monthly fee based on the number of courts: €49 for 1 to 4 courts, €89 for 5 to 8 and €129 for 9 to 12 courts. From 13 courts there is an individual offer. Relays and electric locks are not included and are installed by your electrician. All prices net.

Who is the offer intended for?

Our vending machine offer is aimed exclusively at businesses, clubs and the public sector. Typical operators are padel halls, tennis clubs with padel courts, hotels and leisure facilities that want to offer rackets, balls, snacks and drinks around the clock. Our machines can be found in Memmingen, Straubing and Mainburg, among other places; more in our References.
