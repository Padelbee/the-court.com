    FAQ

    Questions about our projects.

    How many projects has THE COURT completed?

    Our project map shows 50 projects, from Garbsen near Hanover to Graz in Austria. Each dot is a facility that we have advised on, planned or built, managed from Unterföhring near Munich. We show some of them here with photos and details; projects in planning are marked as such.

    What types of facilities have you built?

    Our references range from outdoor courts at tennis clubs to padel halls run by commercial operators. One example is H30 Fitness & Padel in Memmingen with four double and two single courts in a fully refurbished brewery hall. 0711 Padel in Stuttgart stands on a mobile platform. Use the filters above to sort by padel, pickleball, tennis, hall and vending machines.

    What does "in planning" mean for a project?

    "In planning" means that the facility has not yet been built. We are already supporting these projects, for example with planning, permits or financing, and the opening is still to come. As soon as the facility is finished, we add photos and details. You can read how a project runs from idea to opening in From idea to opening.

    Where can I find THE COURT's vending machines?

    Our rental and vending machines can be found in Memmingen, at the Scheck Club in Unterföhring, at 0711 Padel in Stuttgart, at Donau Padel, at Padel Point in Konradsreuth, at TC BW Neufahrn, in Straubing and at TC GR Mainburg, among other places. With the "Vending machines" filter you can see all of these projects. You'll find our full range on the Vending machines page.

    How does my project become the next reference?

    Your project starts with a free initial consultation. In it, we assess the space, target group and potential of your site and discuss budget and financing. Then we plan the courts, foundation and, if needed, a hall, and build them with our partners. Our guide to costs, process and financing shows you what it costs.
