FAQ

Questions about our events.

Where does the Rafa Nadal Padel Tour 2026 stop?

In 2026 the Rafa Nadal Padel Tour stops at three locations in Germany: in Munich at the Scheck Club, in Düsseldorf at Mitte Padel and in Berlin at Padel Ludwigsfelde. Each tour stop brings together players, clubs and operators. If you want to be part of one of the stops, write to us via the contact form.

How does the Best-of-Three-Challenge work?

The Best-of-Three-Challenge is a rapid format: the team that wins 2 points first wins the match. It is played as a simple knockout, so if you lose, you're out. This creates lots of short, exciting matches in a short time. The winning team takes home €1,000 in prize money.

When is f.re.e 2027 in Munich?

f.re.e Munich 2027 takes place from 10 to 14 February 2027 at Messe München. It is the major consumer fair for travel and leisure. Together with MPM GmbH and Sports Connected, we are bringing racket sports to the fair. You'll find all the information about the fair on the official f.re.e website.

What do you do at Active Connected?

At Active Connected, we run the racket sports area, in cooperation with MPM GmbH and Sports Connected. As at f.re.e, we bring racket sports to where players, clubs and operators come together. The date for the next edition will follow; we will add it to this page as soon as it is confirmed.

Can my club take part in an event?

Yes, feel free to get in touch if your club wants to take part in one of our events. Whether it's a tournament, a trade fair or your own format at your facility, we'll work out together what suits your club. Just write to us via the contact form and tell us briefly about your facility.
