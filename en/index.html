FAQ

Questions about your padel facility.

What does THE COURT do?

THE COURT is a project developer for padel, pickleball and tennis facilities in Germany, Austria and Switzerland – from consulting and financing through planning and construction to digitalisation and operation. You have one point of contact for every phase. For your project, we select the right partners from more than 30 in planning, court and hall construction, digitalisation and equipment.

How much does a padel facility cost?

For an initial calculation you need three figures: a padel court costs from €27,000 net, the foundation roughly €35,000 as a guide value and a hall from €280/m² net. On top of that come planning, permits, lighting and digitalisation. The exact amount depends on location, variant and equipment. You will find all the components in the guide to costs, process and financing.

Who do you build padel facilities for?

We build for clubs, hotels, businesses and the public sector. This ranges from the tennis club converting unused courts into padel courts to the commercial operator with its own hall. Besides padel, we also plan facilities for pickleball and tennis. Our offer is aimed exclusively at businesses, clubs and the public sector, not at private individuals.

How does a padel project work?

With us, a padel project runs in seven stages: consulting, financing, planning and architecture, construction, digitalisation, operation and, if desired, franchise. At the start we assess the space, target group and potential of your site. We then clarify financing and permits before construction begins. You can read what happens in each stage in From idea to opening.

Which countries are you active in?

We are active in Germany, Austria and Switzerland. Our 50 projects range from Garbsen to Graz and are managed from Unterföhring near Munich. They include facilities we have advised on, planned or built, from tennis clubs to commercial operators. You can see all locations on the project map and in our references.

Can my tennis club get padel without its own investment?

Yes, through the joint venture with the Motions Racket Club. Your club provides the space and receives a lease payment in return. Construction, financing and operation are handled by the joint venture; for the club, the investment is €0. Your members play with a 30% discount. We explain how the model works on the Motions Racket Club – Joint Venture page.

How do I start my project?

You start with a free initial consultation. In it, we talk about your site, your budget and your usage concept and clarify whether padel is a good fit there. We then run the numbers for your facility and show you which courts, which foundation and which financing are possible. Just write to us via the contact form or by e-mail.
