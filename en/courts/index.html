Foundation

The right foundation.

Fixed concrete slab or mobile, removable platform: the choice depends on the location and the term of your lease. Both options are in a similar price range.

Fixed foundation Long-term site

Permanently poured concrete slab with strip foundation (standard 40 × 80 cm, adapted to the ground, founded below frost depth). Maximum stability, designed for many years of operation. Playing surface made of drainage concrete, porous asphalt or an elastic base layer.

Mobile platform Temporary site

Modular substructure without a concrete foundation. Removable and relocatable, ideal for shorter leases and interim use. Mobile foundations and courts can often be financed through leasing. Currently not eligible for funding in many funding programmes; check case by case.

FAQ

Questions about padel courts.

What padel court variants are there?

There are three variants: Standard, Panoramic and Full Panoramic. They differ mainly in the struts between the glass panels. Standard is particularly stable and economical, Panoramic offers larger glass surfaces and better views, and Full Panoramic is the show court for indoor clubs and tournaments. Each variant is available as a double and as a single court. More on this in the comparison of the three variants.

How much does a padel court cost?

A padel court costs from €27,000 net. The final price depends on the variant, manufacturer and equipment, such as artificial turf, lighting and branding. For the overall calculation, add the foundation at a guide value of around €35,000 and, if needed, a hall from €280/m² net. Our guide to costs, process and financing gives you an overview of all costs.

Which court manufacturers do you work with?

We work with five manufacturers: Padel Concept, 100% Padel, Padel Factory, The Player's Court and Premium Padel Courts. We are not tied to any single manufacturer and compare quality, delivery time, manufacturer's warranty and price for you. For outdoors, Padel Concept and Padel Factory are our favourites; for halls, 100% Padel. More in Five manufacturers, one point of contact.

How much warranty is there on a padel court?

Depending on the manufacturer, there is up to 15 years' manufacturer's warranty on the structure (according to the manufacturer). The 15 years are stated by Padel Factory; for the mobile platform, the manufacturers' figures range from 2 to 15 years. The warranty is always granted by the respective manufacturer, not by THE COURT. We check with the manufacturer which conditions apply to your project.

Fixed foundation or mobile platform: which is better?

That depends mainly on your site and the term of your rental or lease agreement. The fixed foundation is a concrete slab with strip foundation for long-term sites. The mobile platform needs no concrete and can be dismantled and reassembled, ideal for interim use. Both are in a similar price range, with a guide value of around €35,000. More in The foundation.

How much space does a padel hall need?

A polycarbonate hall needs 12 × 21 m, i.e. 252 m², for one court and 26 × 50 m for four courts. The insulated ThermoRoof hall needs 13 × 27 m for one court and 28 × 51 m for four courts. Width, length and height are flexible, and both halls can be extended. Prices start at €280/m² net. All dimensions are in the hall comparison.

Can I give my court my own design?

Yes, your court can be adapted to your brand. Steel and details are available in any RAL colour, the artificial turf in many colours, for example blue, green, terracotta or petrol. Logos are possible on the net, net band, doors and net posts. You also choose light poles, colour temperature and additional LEDs. We coordinate everything with the manufacturer.
